# Publication scope

## Permitted paper story

The viable paper story is now deliberately narrower: a reproducible,
code-to-paper measurement audit of the released CoDE-Stop forced-answer path.
It may:

1. Reconstruct the named released terminal behavior, 21-token cap, and score
   formula.
2. Show that the released endpoint can extend beyond a completed candidate
   answer, so its measurement span can mix candidate and later continuation.
3. Report Q0–Q7 and M1 evidence under their stated backend/sample limits.
4. Compare that released endpoint to an exact outer-box candidate boundary as
   a measurement instrument, not a novel score or controller.
5. Transparently report M2's runtime-feasibility abort and its absence of a
   confirmatory conclusion.
6. Report L1's version-pinned, synthetic equation-to-execution audit as a
   formal reproducibility finding: the released default degeneration path is
   mathematically a recency-weighted count of strict drops in floor-clamped
   log confidence, with separate warm-up, ramp, terminal-gate, and
   strict-comparator semantics.

## Evidence boundary

Q0–Q7 and M1 are supported only to the extent documented by their frozen
artifacts. The Q6 79.37% finding is a counterfactual reduction in trial-probe
generated tokens on that holdout; it is not total inference saving. Q3’s strong
BAC ranking is exploratory, because the independent Q6 holdout had no correct
probe candidates. Q7 shows that Q8 is a stronger practical fidelity backend
than Q4 for the observed fixed-prefix candidates, not that Q4 generalizes to
BF16. M1 is a 16-example development-feasibility study, and its frozen
analyzer's reporting omissions must remain visible.

M2's 100-example confirmation protocol did not reach its 80-valid-pair
minimum after the Q8 runtime stalled. It must be reported as runtime
infeasibility, not as a positive result, a null result, or a basis to alter
the M2 protocol.

L1 is non-empirical. Its synthetic fixtures establish formal distinctions in
the named pinned release, not an empirical change in decisions, confidence,
cost, calibration, accuracy, or safety. It cannot be used to describe a
changed equation as an improved CoDE-Stop plug-in.

## Relationship to prior work

CoDE-Stop supplies the released checkpoint-and-forced-answer setting.
REFRAIN already uses answer-only boxed-region geometric-mean likelihood, so
the paper cannot claim that score or generic forced-answer stopping as novel.
Conformal Thinking and PUMA further limit broader method claims. The final
paper must state this boundary and disclose any subsequently found direct
overlap work.

## Publication standard

The contribution is not a claim of a new state of the art, improved CoDE-Stop
accuracy, a faithful BF16 reproduction, a TMLR submission, an accepted
publication, or a validated new stopping method. P1 is paused, and M2 does
not establish a confirmatory generalization result. No further empirical study
is authorized by this public record.
